## 2. Requirements

### 2.1 Pricing and delivery value (F-001)

| ID    | Requirement                                                                                           | Acceptance criteria                                                                                          |
| ----- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| FIN-1 | Each product has a unit price, set by the Bakery Admin.                                               | A product without a price cannot be delivered; price is a whole number of won.                               |
| FIN-2 | Financial amounts use the actual delivered quantity, never the ordered quantity.                      | Example: ordered Bread 30, delivered Bread 20 at 1,000 won gives 20,000 won.                                 |
| FIN-3 | The system calculates a total for each delivery and for each settlement period.                       | Delivery total is the sum of its lines; period total is the sum of the deliveries inside the period.         |
| FIN-4 | Financial records stay connected to delivery records.                                                 | Every amount traces to a delivery line; opening a delivery shows its value and the payments paid against it. |
| FIN-5 | _Assumption:_ the unit price in force when the delivery is recorded is copied onto the delivery line. | A later price change never changes past deliveries.                                                          |

### 2.4 Payments (F-001a to F-001d)

| ID             | Requirement                                                                                                               | Acceptance criteria                                                                                                |
| -------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| PAY-1 (F-001a) | The contractor submits a payment confirmation with the payment amount and payment proof, such as a bank transfer receipt. | Amount and proof are both required; proof is an image or PDF up to 10 MB (_assumption_).                           |
| PAY-2 (F-001a) | The contractor sees the status of each submission.                                                                        | Statuses: pending, approved, rejected; a rejected one shows the reason.                                            |
| PAY-3 (F-001b) | The Bakery Admin reviews submissions and approves or rejects them.                                                        | Approval records the approved amount and payment date; rejection requires a reason.                                |
| PAY-4 (F-001b) | Submissions stay pending until approved. Only approved payments change the official outstanding balance.                  | Pending and rejected amounts never appear in the balance; they are shown separately as pending.                    |
| PAY-5 (F-001b) | All payment actions are written to the audit history.                                                                     | Submit, approve, reject, allocate and re-allocate each create an audit entry with actor, time, old and new values. |
| PAY-6 (F-001c) | A contractor can pay part of the outstanding balance; the balance is recalculated after approval.                         | Example: 400,000 won delivered, 250,000 paid, 150,000 outstanding.                                                 |
| PAY-7 (F-001d) | One payment can cover several delivery records.                                                                           | The Bakery Admin splits the approved amount across deliveries.                                                     |
| PAY-8 (F-001d) | The system tracks how much has been paid against each delivery.                                                           | Each delivery shows value, paid so far and remaining; allocations to a delivery never exceed its remaining value.  |

### 2.5 Settlement (F-001e to F-001i)

| ID             | Requirement                                                                                | Acceptance criteria                                                                                                                          |
| -------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| SET-1 (F-001e) | Mid-month and month-end settlement reports are available.                                  | Each report shows delivered value for the period, approved payments for the period, and remaining outstanding balance.                       |
| SET-2 (F-001f) | Unpaid balances stay outstanding and carry forward into the next settlement period.        | The next report opens with the previous closing balance; closing a report never erases an unpaid amount; historical payments stay available. |
| SET-3 (F-001g) | The Bakery Admin configures settlement cutoff dates, which may differ from month to month. | Example: November 1 to 12, then November 13 to 28; the next month may use other dates. Reports are calculated from the configured dates.     |
| SET-4 (F-001h) | Each contractor has an independent settlement schedule, managed by the Bakery Admin.       | Two contractors can have different dates; a contractor dashboard shows only its own schedule; the Bakery Admin sees and edits all.           |
| SET-5 (F-001i) | Changing settlement dates applies only to future periods.                                  | A closed report, its payments and its allocations are never changed or recalculated by a schedule change.                                    |
| SET-6 (F-001i) | Schedule changes are recorded in the audit history.                                        | Old dates, new dates, actor and time are stored.                                                                                             |

## 4. Business rules

1.  Delivery value is the sum over its lines of delivered quantity times the unit price copied onto the line. Ordered quantity is never used.

2.  Outstanding balance equals total delivered value minus total approved and allocated payments. Pending and rejected payments are excluded.

3.  Money is stored as whole won (integers); no decimals.

4.  An approved payment must be fully allocated to deliveries; the approved amount may differ from the submitted amount, and the difference is shown. _Assumption._

5.  A delivery cannot receive more allocation than its remaining value.

6.  A rejected submission cannot be edited; the contractor submits a new one. An approved payment cannot be edited; a mistake is fixed by an audited reversal entry. _Assumption._

7.  Settlement periods for one contractor are contiguous: each starts the day after the previous one ends. _Assumption:_ in the example (November 1 to 12, then 13 to 28), November 29 and 30 fall in the next period.

8.  Closing a report stores an unchangeable snapshot: opening balance, delivered value, approved payments, closing balance and the line items.

9.  If a delivery inside a closed period is corrected later (an approved delivery change request), the closed report stays unchanged; the difference becomes an adjustment entry in the current open period. _Assumption._

10. Schedule edits apply only to periods that have not started. _Assumption:_ the end date of the current open period can still be moved until it is closed.

11. Every contractor sees only its own rows; isolation is enforced as in the original document.

## 5. Data model

Tables added or changed, all with organization_id and the standard timestamps.

| Table                    | Key fields                                                                                                                                                                             | Notes                                                    |
| ------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- |
| product_prices           | id, product_id, unit_price, effective_from, created_by                                                                                                                                 | Price history; current price is the latest effective one |
| delivery_lines (changed) | adds unit_price, line_value                                                                                                                                                            | Copied when the delivery is recorded                     |
| deliveries (changed)     | adds total_value                                                                                                                                                                       | Sum of line values, kept in step with edits              |
| payments                 | id, contractor_org_id, submitted_by, submitted_amount, proof_file_url, status (pending, approved, rejected), approved_amount, payment_date, reviewed_by, reviewed_at, rejection_reason | One row per confirmation                                 |
| payment_allocations      | id, payment_id, delivery_id, amount, created_by                                                                                                                                        | Sum per payment equals approved_amount                   |
| settlement_periods       | id, contractor_link_id, start_date, end_date, kind (mid_month, month_end), status (open, closed), created_by                                                                           | One row per period, per contractor                       |
| settlement_reports       | id, period_id, opening_balance, delivered_value, approved_payments, adjustments, closing_balance, snapshot, closed_by, closed_at                                                       | Written once at closing                                  |
| balance_adjustments      | id, contractor_link_id, delivery_id, period_id, amount, reason                                                                                                                         | Corrections after a period is closed                     |
| audit_log (existing)     | reused                                                                                                                                                                                 | Payment, allocation, schedule and closing events         |

Calculations:

```latex
V_d = \sum_{l \in d} q^{delivered}_l \cdot p_l \qquad B = \sum_{d} V_d - \sum_{a \in approved} a
```

Closing balance of a period equals its opening balance plus its delivered value plus adjustments minus its approved payments; the next period's opening balance is this closing balance.

## 8. Test scenarios

- Ordered Bread 30 at 1,000 won, delivered 20: delivery value is 20,000 won.

- Delivered 400,000 won, one approved payment of 250,000: outstanding is 150,000.

- A pending payment of 150,000 leaves the official outstanding at 150,000 until approved.

- A rejection without a reason is refused; a rejected payment never changes a balance.

- One 300,000 payment allocated across three deliveries: each delivery shows its own paid and remaining amounts; allocating more than a delivery's remaining value is refused.

- Close the period November 1 to 12 with 150,000 unpaid: the next period opens at 150,000 and the closed report is unchanged.

- Change a contractor's dates for the next month: the closed report and its allocations are byte-for-byte unchanged and an audit entry exists.

- Contractor A has dates November 1 to 12 and 13 to 28, contractor B has other dates: each sees only their own; the admin sees both.

- A contractor cannot read another contractor's payments or proof files (403 or 404).

- Price changed from 1,000 to 1,200 won after a delivery: the old delivery value does not change.

- A delivery corrected after its period closed creates an adjustment in the open period and leaves the closed report unchanged.

- Every payment action and schedule change appears in the audit history.
